# World and lore

Humans only. Dune vibes. No gods, no chosen one, no superweapon. Names are two layers: imperial map names + worker nicknames (Saltgate, the Drip).

## The Severance

60–80 years ago, within living memory. The empire pulled its fleets home for a civil war, then the outbound rifts went dark. Cause unknown: the central mystery. "Abandoned, not dead."

## Rifts

Jump points are natural rifts. They can't be built, only stabilized; the empire's gate stations sit on them. Rifts are the only way between stars (sublight = decades). Jumping = a few seconds of charge near the rift. More connected = more chaotic.

## Map (mirror)

Federation pocket (behind the Wall) | the Crossroads (Dynasty chokepoint gate, "Constantinople") | Dynasty heartland pocket.
Travel only along drawn lanes (`sector_map.gd` `REGIONS`, `_net()`). Federation's only outside lane is the Wall → Crossroads. The Remnant has no Crossroads lane; reach it only via League or Dynasty.

## Powers

| Tier | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Great | **Federation** (NCR-like republic, the "good guys", bureaucratic, overstretched; charters the Charter Company), **Dynasty** (young Ottoman-style conquerors, ~3 generations; wise aging Suleiman-like ruler, heirs circling; clan fleets vs loyal gate guard), **Merchant League** (Venice-like lane thalassocracy, buys claims), **the Faithful** (zealots at the dead rifts), **Imperial Remnant** (Byzantium-like holdout) |
| Minor | Free Ports, Miners' Compact (neutral by Federation–Dynasty treaty; League never signed), Dynasty-border clan states, nomad ark fleets |
| Outlaw | Pirate clans (some sponsored as corsairs), smugglers, mercs, deserters. Player can rise to minor power |

## Neon colors (dialogue)

People white, places hot magenta, threats neon red, Charter sodium amber, Navy cobalt, Dynasty Ottoman green (extra glow), Tally's crew ultraviolet in his handwriting, credits gold.

## Pillars

- Be anyone: merchant, pirate, privateer, collaborator, miner, explorer, warlord.
- Everything real-time; no separate battle screens.
- Named characters are captured or wounded, rarely killed.
