# Story and cast

Tone: grounded grey with hope, told from below ("this game is for the proletariat"). Found family is a pillar: hiring two characters can reveal a hidden bond (Mersham + Fay).
Voice bible: https://claude.ai/artifact/6EQS3CahnD1EPzZjATyuX1 (Firewatch radio, Katana Zero menace, M&B companions). Script doc wins over `DRAFT` lines in `data.gd`.

## Starts

Every start is a debt story with its own main enemy. Tally and Franz are cross-start characters; past a point of no return they're mutually exclusive.

| Start | Status | Hook | Enemy | Allegiance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Merchant | Playable | Space trucker, rig loan to the Charter Company; pay off = owner-operator, papers, the Wall | Charter Company | Locked to Navy (`Career.TALLY_FORK = false`) |

## Merchant opening beats

1. Sign the loan, fill the ID form, name Dez on the emergency-contact form (pick his background)
2. Water run, Drip → Greenhouse. Alejandra Vargas (Sweet Margaret) flies with you and coaches; Hollis Gray's rig "Hollis' Girl" is raided ahead
3. Meet Dez at the Greenhouse
4. First jump into Marcellus: Tallyman ambush (unwinnable, nobody dies), white flare, hail, he spares you; Franz's patrol arrives
5. Four: Franz counts Tally's marks, posts the Red Stork bounty (pilot Qasim), lends marines, later the Navy offer (PO Mbeki, FNS Ulysses)
6. Saltgate bar: Fay, Mersham, Alejandra, Hollis
7. Pay off → papers → customs at the Wall → jump → the Crossroads under the Dynasty fortress → one line → title card

Forks (each pays off at the gate and carries forward): help or rob rigs (lanes remember you), Navy or Tallyman, bounty alive or dead (Dynasty paymaster thread, hunter Trojan), no-questions crates (paymaster at Amber's).

## Cast

- **Dez (Desmond Okafor)**: old friend, wisecracking conscience, free escort, never leaves. Backgrounds: rift pilot, ex-navy gunner, yard mechanic, dock hustler.
- **Charlie (Charlotte Hansen)**: Charter dispatch. Kind voice for a greedy boss. "Officially / unofficially."
- **Lt. Franz Kessler**: young, earnest navy officer from a mining family. Never takes bribes. Main pull to the Navy.
- **Jack Tallyn, the Tallyman**: ex-Charter trucker broken by debt. Hits Charter rigs only, lets crews walk, tallies each rig. Dark mirror. Franz/Tally = Heat: mutual respect, collision course.
- **Fay (Phaedra Andreou)**: one of Durda's scrap kids, stowed away to Saltgate. Cheap, desperate to see the gate, scales into any role.
- **Mersham Ali of Clan Karabey**: Dynasty defector, stuck. Ace, leads from the front, "ultimate dad vibes". First real commander.
- **Durda Reeves**: Scrapper boss. Greasy, Penguin vibe, only loves money. Kids carry debts on his books.
- **Captain Rex / Perfido**: Pirate start betrayer. Griffith-like.
- Minor: Alejandra Vargas, Hollis Gray, PO Mbeki, Qasim, Notch (Tally raider), Trojan (hunter), Nell (Saltgate).

## Scrapper (locked decisions)

Wreckyard Nine, Marcellus belt. Goal: keep the ship (buy her free or bolt). Cold open stripping a fresh wreck before Charter Recovery. Salvage = tractor drifting pieces with the mouse. Companions = survivors you pull out. Bolting: Reeves sells your debt to the Charter (repo cutter). Sell to Reeves (lowball) or Amber's fence (double, he hears). Best salvage in the Drift (past the beacon ring). Ending: you paint her name; whoever you mattered to hails you by it. Title card.

## Slang (Hesper March)

"Grye" = trucker greeting ("Grye, hauler."): bare grey hull, one of us. Red paint = repossessed. "Mint" = make money. "Family" = fellow liened truckers. No borrowed slang.
