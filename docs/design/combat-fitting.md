# Combat, fitting, crew

Stance: the player's strength is as a **captain**, not just the flagship. Orders make you great; firepower comes from outfitting.

## Flight (locked)

Newtonian, hard top speed per hull, ship-relative thrusters (W/S, A/D strafe, Q/E turn), camera never rotates, X brake assist, mouse steering + flight assist on by default. Engine outfits change thrust only. No cruise, no boost, no time skip.

## Weapons

- Left click = turrets (MG tracers, flak), Space = forward cannons (blasters), right click = secondary (ion bolt, torpedo; R swaps).
- Triangle by range: cannons (~900) beat turret ships (~600); turrets shred fighters; fighters harass cannon ships.
- Turret traverse is set by the hull. Merchant freighter is **turrets only**.
- Shields absorb first and recharge out of fire; armor adds hull + mass. Built-in reactor + energy bar, no heat.
- Ship is disabled below ~15% hull; keep shooting and it dies. Escorts cease fire on disabled ships.

## Fitting

Endless Sky space pools: outfit space with weapon/engine sub-limits, fixed gun and turret mount counts per hull. No tiers or random modifiers. Opening utilities: cargo pod, crew quarters, armory, scanner.

## Fleet orders (Tab wheel, time slows)

Engage / Focus / Screen me / Bait / Break off; Wedge / Line / Ring. Each captain obeys in their own temper (`Data.captains`). **No talking during engagements**: comms queue until the fight ends.

## Boarding

Disable, then a plan card at half speed (Storm the bridge / Seal the crew in) and a ~10 s live breach over the ship. Bigger ships need more marines, not worse odds. Lawful boarding pays (bounty alive > dead, prize court cut). Should feel juicy, not scale to fleets.

## Crew v1 (scope locked, waits on Vishnu's playtest)

- Two tiers, M&B style: **crew** (numbers) and **captains** (named).
- Spacer = recruit tier, trains into Gunner / Engineer / Marine / Pilot (or hire specialists at bars, pricier).
- Ranks green → veteran → elite: bonus ×1/×1.5/×2, wages ×1/×2/×4. Spacer 40/wk, Gunner/Engineer/Pilot 90, Marine 120.
- Station slots cap bonuses (no stacking). Spare berths always exist; spares fight boardings at half.
- Casualties wound (sit out a week) or kill. Morale from pay, wins, loot, losses.
- Origins: Charter wage hands, Compact miners (engineers), Navy deserters (gunners), Dynasty exiles (marines).
- Start = 2 green Charter spacers. Prisoners: recruit or ransom.
- Look: ledger manifest page with ID portraits + compact party list on the HUD.
